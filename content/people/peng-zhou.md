## Biography

Dr. Peng Zhou leads the **General Manipulation Intelligence (GMI) Lab** at Great Bay University. His research brings together robotics, machine learning, and computer vision to develop robots that interact with the physical world with skill, adaptability, and generality.

He received his Ph.D. in Robotics from **The Hong Kong Polytechnic University** (2019–2022), advised by David Navarro-Alarcon, and subsequently worked as a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at **The University of Hong Kong** with Jia Pan. He also visited Stanford University’s Robotics Lab with Oussama Khatib (2016) and KTH’s Robotics, Perception and Learning Lab with Danica Kragic (2021).

## Research interests

His current work focuses on **dexterous and dynamic manipulation**, **agentic mobile manipulation**, and **deformable object manipulation**. The lab combines multimodal perception, latent dynamics and world models, robot foundation models, and teleoperation to connect perception, prediction, planning, learning, and control.
