The **General Manipulation Intelligence (GMI) Lab** at Great Bay University develops robots that interact with the physical world with *skill, adaptability, and generality*. Our research centres on **dexterous and dynamic manipulation**, **agentic mobile manipulation**, and **deformable object manipulation**.

Our methods combine **multimodal perception**, integrating vision, tactile, and force sensing, with **latent dynamics models and world models**, **robot foundation models**, and **teleoperation**. By connecting perception, prediction, planning, learning, and control, we aim to develop embodied agents that acquire transferable manipulation skills and act with greater **intelligent autonomy**.

Explore our [publications](/publications/) to learn more.
